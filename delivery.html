II.

Returns

Not satisfied? No problem. By law you have 14 days from delivery to cancel any order for any reason (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013), and we voluntarily extend this to a full thirty days to change your mind. You're welcome to inspect an item as you would in a shop; we simply ask that it comes back in resaleable condition where possible. Return postage for a change of mind is at your cost; if an item is faulty, we cover it.

  1. 01

    Write to us

    Send an email to support@shopcienty.com with your order number and a brief note. No forms, no portals.

  2. 02

    Receive your label

    We'll reply with a prepaid Royal Mail return label, usually within the same working day.

  3. 03

    Pack & post

    Pack the item securely and drop it at any Royal Mail collection point, at your convenience.

  4. 04

    Refund issued

    Once received and inspected, your refund is processed within 5–7 business days, to the original method of payment.

✦

Damaged or faulty on arrival?

If anything arrives in less-than-perfect condition, write to us straight away — a photograph helps us sort it faster. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you may reject faulty goods within 30 days for a full refund, or ask for a replacement. Either way we cover return postage.
