Dr Beckmann Stain Devils Cosmetics & Nature is a specialist stain remover designed for the toughest pigment-based marks — from lipstick, foundation and mascara to grass, mud, soil and pollen. It tackles the stubborn cosmetic and outdoor stains that everyday washing alone often can't shift, making it a handy treatment for clothes and household fabrics. The formula combines Dr Beckmann's pigment remover technology with surfactants to break down and lift stain particles, helping to keep colours bright and fibres intact. It works as a pre-treatment before machine washing, and can also be dabbed gently onto items that cannot be washed. To use, apply directly onto the stain, leave to work for a few minutes, then wash as normal. For non-washable items, dab gently and blot with a clean, damp cloth. Suitable for cotton, synthetics and blended fabrics, and free from chlorine bleach. Each 50ml bottle is compact enough for a handbag, desk drawer, gym bag or suitcase. This pack of 2 gives you one to keep at home and one to take with you, so a stain solution is always to hand. The bottle is recyclable* (*where facilities exist).
