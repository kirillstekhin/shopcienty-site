03 / THE PROPOSAL

Better pages.
A shared commercial interest.

We are a retailer. We would do the agreed catalogue work without a service fee in return for authorisation to sell Flava It! on Amazon UK.

WHAT WE WOULD DO

Put the catalogue in order.

  • Map the 82 pages found and recommend priorities.
  • Prepare approved titles, product details and imagery for an agreed first batch.
  • Assess appropriate variations, duplicates, A+ content and the Store.
  • Agree the wider rollout and ongoing checks after reviewing the first batch.
WHAT WE WOULD NEED

Your authorisation and product facts.

  • A letter authorising Shopcienty to sell on Amazon UK, if you decide to proceed.
  • Current pack artwork, specifications and permission to use the supplied content.
  • An agreed way to submit changes: through your team or separately granted permissions. No shared passwords.
  • Your approval before each batch is submitted.

No service fee does not mean an unlimited commitment. We would agree the page list, deliverables, timing and responsibilities in writing before work begins. We are not asking for exclusivity.
