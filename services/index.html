What we do

A

Listing image set with compliance check

A main image that meets Amazon's image policy, plus six infographics built from what customers actually search for — benefits, how to use, size and contents, comparison, lifestyle. Each image is checked before delivery: white background, 85 % fill, no text on the main image, no claim the pack doesn't support.

From £60 (main image + 2 infographics) · £150 for the 7-image set with compliance report · £290 with A+ (5 modules) and rewritten copy. 48–72 hours.

B

Listing audit & fix

A listing that is live but not selling usually has three to five fixable problems: an image that violates policy, claims Amazon's systems flag, missing search terms, a suppressed variation, a brand or ASIN conflict. We find them against the published rules and tell you exactly what to change — or apply the fixes through a user you create in your own Seller Central.

£99 for one ASIN (10 findings with fixes) · £190 for up to five · £390 with fixes applied and a before/after report.

C

Seller automation

The checks we run for our own account every morning, built for yours: stock and days of cover from SP-API, Buy Box status, ads spend and ACoS, a daily digest to email or Telegram, repricing checks for eBay from supplier price lists. Built in n8n, Make, Google Sheets or Claude — in your accounts, with your data, documented so you can change it later.

£150 for one documented workflow · £450 for up to three connected workflows with two weeks of support · £150/month maintenance.

How we work

  1. 1

    You brief us once.

    Product photos or the ASIN, the pack text, your target search terms if you have them. For an audit — the listing link is enough.

  2. 2

    A draft within 72 hours.

    Images, copy or a workflow, with a plain-English note of what was wrong and what we changed.

  3. 3

    Nothing is pushed to your account without your sign-off. Source files are included.

04

A good fit, and what we don't do

Who this is for

Small and mid-size sellers on Amazon UK, US and EU with 1–50 ASINs; brands moving onto Amazon for the first time; eBay and Etsy sellers who need consistent images at volume.

What we won't do

Put another brand's logo on a main image without the brand's permission. Invent reviews or badges for slides. Make claims the pack doesn't support. Promise sales results — we promise a compliant, complete listing and a clear report.
